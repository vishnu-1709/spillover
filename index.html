Three ways to play

The host picks the mode, the number of rounds, and which surprise events can show up.

Duel 2 players

One on one. Dodge their shots, land yours, and knock out their ship first. Best of 3, 5 or 7 rounds.

Team 4 or 6 players

Two sides, facing each other. Shots fly across to the other team, and your ship can slide onto a teammate's screen to help them out.

Bomb 3 to 6 players

One bomb, one burning fuse. Catch it, throw it to the next phone, and don't be holding it when it goes off. Grenades and mines keep everyone moving.

Events

The host can switch on events that hit mid-round: Lights out, Flip, Shrink and Hot hands. Only the ones they pick will show up.

Tournaments

Got 3 to 8 people? Run a 1v1 knockout bracket. Everyone not playing watches the match live on their own phone.

Installing it

  1. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome warns that the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the file from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway.

The warnings appear because the beta isn't from the Play Store yet, not because anything is wrong with the file.

Getting an update later? Download the new version and install it over the old one. Your progress stays.
