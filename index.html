Ready in a minute

Everyone needs the game on their own phone. That's all the setup there is.

  1. 1

    Join the same Wi-Fi

    All phones on one network. A phone's hotspot works too, so you can play anywhere.

  2. 2

    One hosts, the rest join

    Open the game and tap Play. One person makes a room and picks the mode. Everyone else taps it in the list.

  3. 3

    Put the phones top to top

    Top edges touching, flat on a table, or hold them and sit facing each other. With more players, sit in a circle.

  4. 4

    Tilt to move, tap to shoot

    Hold to charge a bigger shot. The round button in the corner fires your power.

Three ways to play

The host picks the mode, the number of rounds, and which surprise events can show up.

Duel 2 players

One on one. Dodge their shots, land yours, and knock out their ship first. Best of 3, 5 or 7 rounds.

Bomb 3 to 6 players

One bomb, one burning fuse. Catch it, throw it to the next phone, and don't be holding it when it goes off. Grenades and mines keep everyone moving.

Team 4 or 6 players

Two sides, facing each other. Shots fly across to the other team, and your ship can slide onto a teammate's screen to help them out.

Events

The host can switch on events that hit mid-round: Lights out, Flip, Shrink and Hot hands. Only the ones they pick will show up.

Tournaments

Got 3 to 8 people? Run a 1v1 knockout bracket. Everyone not playing watches the match live on their own phone.

Pick your ship

Three ships that play very differently. Each one comes in two looks, so pick the one that feels like yours.

Shooter

Fast, straight shots. The easiest to pick up and the hardest to master.

Scatter

Fires a spread of shots. Slower, short range, and brutal up close.

Venom

Lobs poison. A miss isn't wasted: it leaves a puddle that hurts anyone who moves through it.

Plus one power each round: Shield Dash Jam Magnet

Installing it

  1. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome warns that the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the file from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway.

The warnings appear because the beta isn't from the Play Store yet, not because anything is wrong with the file.

Getting an update later? Download the new version and install it over the old one. Your coins and unlocks stay.
